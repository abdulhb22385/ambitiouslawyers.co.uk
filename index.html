This website does not contain a newsletter signup form or collect subscription details directly. Subscriptions to The Ambitious Lawyers Brief are made and managed on LinkedIn. LinkedIn processes subscription information under its own privacy policy. You can manage or cancel your subscription on LinkedIn.

6. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law gives you rights to access, correct or erase personal information, restrict or object to processing, and request data portability. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent.

For information you provide directly to us, email info@ambitiouslawyers.co.uk. For information processed by LinkedIn, use its privacy controls and contact channels. You may also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office.

7. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as the website or our practices change. The date above will reflect the latest revision.
